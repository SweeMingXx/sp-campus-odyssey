# Usability and production review

Implemented against Nielsen heuristics:
1. Status: loading state, interaction prompts, objective status, XP/stamina and save notifications.
2. Familiar language: campus roles, named facilities, recognisable controls and offices.
3. Control: Escape/back, cancel import/reset, clear waypoints and recovery to spawn.
4. Consistency: shared typography, palette, buttons, cards and navigation.
5. Prevention: destructive-action confirmation, bounded saves and duplicate reward protection.
6. Recognition: searchable directory, visible controls, minimap and project tracking.
7. Efficiency: keyboard shortcuts, pointer-lock fallback, touch and quality settings.
8. Minimal design: focused HUD with contextual detail in menus.
9. Recovery: constructive wrong-answer feedback, corrupt-save handling and graphics recovery.
10. Help: in-game guide, role descriptions, sources and deployment documentation.

Before production approval: verify browser CI, review screenshots, test actual mobile hardware and Safari/Firefox, conduct real-user accessibility testing, validate content with campus stakeholders and profile sustained GPU/memory use. This checklist is not a usability study or accessibility certification.
